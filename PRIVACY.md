# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

20-20-20-eyecare is a simple eye-care timer app.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect personal data.

## Tracking

The app does not track users across apps or websites.

## Data Sharing

The app does not share personal data with third parties.

## Local Settings

The app may store timer preferences, language preferences, and run mode settings locally on your device. These settings are used only to make the app work as expected and are not sent to the developer.

## Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, the iPhone app may sync basic app preferences, such as language and run mode, to the Apple Watch app. This data stays within your devices.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, please use the GitHub repository:

https://github.com/q7jxb7yxdk-star/20-20-20
